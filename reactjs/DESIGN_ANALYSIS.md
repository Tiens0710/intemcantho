# Intemcantho.vn Design Analysis

## Key Visual Elements

### Hero Section
- **Layout**: Left side text + right side professional photo of woman in white shirt
- **Text on left**: Large orange italic text "IN TEM" + "Cần thơ" + "NHANH ĐẸP" with decorative lines
- **Right side**: Professional portrait photo in circular/oval frame with orange gradient background
- **Decorative elements**: Small circles, dots, and geometric shapes scattered around
- **Stats boxes**: "800+ deliveries per day", "1000+ satisfied customers" on the right side
- **CTA button**: "Bắt ngỏ" (Start now) in orange/brown

### Color Scheme
- **Primary**: Warm orange/brown (#C97D5C or similar)
- **Secondary**: Light beige/cream background
- **Text**: Dark brown/charcoal for body text
- **Accents**: Soft orange for headings and highlights

### Typography
- **Logo**: "intem cần thơ" - italic, orange, stylized
- **Main heading**: Large orange italic text with decorative elements
- **Section titles**: Orange color, uppercase
- **Body text**: Dark brown, readable sans-serif

### Services Section ("DỊCH VỤ CỦA DUKY")
- **Layout**: 6 service cards in a grid
- **Each card has**:
  - Icon/image at top
  - Service name in orange
  - Brief description
- **Services**: Brochure, Stand, Labels, Flyers, Packaging, Business Cards

### Why Choose Us Section
- **Layout**: 6 feature boxes with icons
- **Features displayed**: Consulting, Transparent pricing, Creative design, Modern equipment, Clear timeline, Quality control
- **Each box has**: Icon + heading + description

### Testimonials Section
- **Layout**: Quote cards with customer names and roles
- **Design**: Simple, clean cards with quoted text

### Process Section
- **Layout**: 5 steps in a timeline
- **Visual**: Numbers in circles connected by a line

### Overall Design Philosophy
- Clean, professional, modern
- Warm, approachable color palette
- Good use of whitespace
- Professional photography
- Clear hierarchy and organization
- Decorative geometric elements for visual interest
